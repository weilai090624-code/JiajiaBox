===== sing-box-lx (VLESS Encryption client) =====

Project: https://github.com/Leadaxe/sing-box-lx
Branch/commit used for the port: lx / 9c655e3
License: GNU General Public License v3.0 or later.

JiajiaBox incorporates the client-side VLESS Encryption implementation from
this fork (protocol/vless/encryption and related wiring), itself derived from
the sing-box fork at github.com/starifly/sing-box.

Only the client side is included. The upstream implementation states that
native.0rtt was verified against a live server; xorpub, random and 1rtt modes
are parsed/constructed but not device-verified.
